- Starting corpus: ₹10 crore
- Monthly withdrawal: ₹3.5 lakh (₹42 lakh/year), withdrawn at year-end
- Return scenarios: 10%, 12%, 14% annualized

## Suggested aggressive allocation (illustrative)
- 70% Equity index/core (Nifty 50 + Nifty Next 50 + Flexicap blend)
- 15% Mid/Small cap tilt
- 10% International equity (US/global index)
- 5% Gold
- Keep 24 months of withdrawals in debt/liquid bucket (outside growth bucket)

## Risk controls
- Annual rebalancing with ±5% bands
- Guardrail: if portfolio falls >20% from peak, cut discretionary spend by 10–15%
- Cap single-stock/speculative exposure to <=10% total portfolio
- Tax optimization: prefer low-turnover funds, use tax-loss harvesting
